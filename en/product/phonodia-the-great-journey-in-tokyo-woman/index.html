A statement T-shirt inspired by Japanese culture, the power of nature and the poetry of travel. ThePHONODIA – The Great Journey in Tokyo (July 2025)is the commemorative merch of PHONODIA's great journey to the Tokyo International Choir Competition in July 2025.

The front is dominated by an artistic rendering of a cherry tree (sakura) in vivid red and black, a symbol of rebirth, of beauty and of the fleeting moment. On the back, the distinctive red circle inspired by the Japanese sun frames the cherry blossom, together with typography in English, Japanese and Greek, creating a powerful visual and emotional imprint.

The phrase
«I hold cherry blossoms and I tell you I love you»
adds a poetic dimension and makes the T-shirt unique and collectable.

Features:

  • Unisex cut
  • 100% cotton
  • Hard-wearing print
  • A comfortable, modern fit
