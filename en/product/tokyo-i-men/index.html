A T-shirt that brings together Japanese aesthetics, a poetic mood and contemporary design. ThePHONODIA in Tokyois the collector's keepsake of PHONODIA's great journey to the Tokyo International Choir Competition in July 2025, inspired by the cherry blossom and the magic of Tokyo.

On the front, a discreet graphic at the chest with Japanese characters and sakura; on the back, striking artwork of cherry blossom, with typography in Latin, Japanese and Greek characters, breathing emotion and artistic identity.

Made from high-quality cotton, it offers a comfortable fit and a soft handle, ideal for everyday wear as well as for special occasions.

Features:

  • Unisex cut
  • 100% cotton
  • Hard-wearing print
  • A comfortable, modern fit
